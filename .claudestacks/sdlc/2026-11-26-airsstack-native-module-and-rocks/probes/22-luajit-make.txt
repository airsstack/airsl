- Backend picked 'luajit': CARGO-WRAPPER args=build --release --package airsstack-native --target-dir=target --features luajit.
- luajit -e require -> 'table 42 true nil LuaJIT 2.1.1770848219' (module loads, jit feature compiled in, no global).
- Installed .so at lib/lua/5.1/airsstack.so; rock dir rocks-5.1/airsstack-luajit. Package name airsstack-luajit with module airsstack works (B9).
- SURPRISE: luarocks-build-rust-mlua was installed under rocks-5.4 + share/lua/5.4 of the same tree (build.lua:128-143 swaps cfg.lua_version to _VERSION of the running interpreter, 5.4). Afterwards 'show' lists it as '(missing)' for 5.1.
- SURPRISE: make ends with a spurious 'Missing dependencies for airsstack-luajit 0.0.1-1: lua 5.1 (not installed)' although exit=0 and the module works. Likely the rocks_provided cache (util.lua get_rocks_provided caches when called without a rockspec) is filled while cfg.lua_version is temporarily 5.4 during the build-deps phase; cosmetic but visible to users.
